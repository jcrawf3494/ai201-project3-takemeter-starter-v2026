analysis: A post that supports its central argument with at least one specific, checkable fact, such as a number, date, technical comparison, or documented event.
hot_take: A post that makes a strong, generalized judgment without presenting specific, checkable evidence to support it.
personal_preference: A post focused primarily on the author's own likes, dislikes, feelings, or personal experiences rather than making a universal claim.
proposal: A post that explicitly recommends changing a rule, policy, standard, or common practice.
